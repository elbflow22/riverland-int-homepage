01Über uns

Authentizität, Qualität und Nachhaltigkeit — direkt aus Asien.

Die Riverland International GmbH ist das Unternehmen hinter der Handelsmarke OG ASIA, unserer Retail-Marke für asiatische Produkte auf Amazon Europa. Wir machen authentische Produkte zugänglich, damit jeder Kultur und Geschmack aus Asien neu entdecken kann — egal, wo er lebt.

Mit einem vielfältigen Sortiment an Lebensmitteln und Marken aus Vietnam, Korea, Japan, China und Thailand steht Riverland für Authentizität, Qualität und Nachhaltigkeit. Wir erweitern kontinuierlich unser E-Commerce-Angebot und fördern den interkulturellen Austausch in Europa.

Unsere Marke

OG ASIA

Unsere Retail-Marke auf Amazon Europa und TikTok Shop. Kuratierte asiatische Lebensmittel und Lifestyle-Produkte — von Sojasaucen und Reisspezialitäten bis hin zu Snacks, Tees und Küchenutensilien.

02Riverland M.A.V.
M.A.V.

Mission (the Why), Aims (the What), and Values (the How) — our north star at Riverland International. The framework that defines why we exist, what we are building, and how we behave along the way.

Riverland M.A.V. — Q3 2025 Deck
M.

Mission

The Why — why we exist

Making authentic products accessible — so everyone can discover and reconnect with culture, no matter where they live.

A.

Aims

The What — our long-term objectives

  1. Enrich marketplaces with authentic products.

  2. Grow the e-commerce selection for Asian groceries.

  3. Advance standards for efficient and sustainable fulfillment processes.

Becoming Europe's Online Asian Shop — by continuously extending our authentic product selection and delivering consistent, reliable quality to create lasting value for our customers and our stakeholders.

V.

Values

The How — our principles for action
  1. 01

    Ownership

    Own your work.

    We trust our judgment, we act with intention, and we hold ourselves accountable to the highest standards.

  2. 02

    Action-bias

    Think fast, act quick.

    We don't wait — we move. We seize momentum, take smart risks, and get things done. Perfection can wait — progress can't. When it's not working, we fix it fast and keep pushing forward.

  3. 03

    Growth-mindset

    Learn from outcomes, keep improving.

    Keeping an open mind and always looking for the truth — it's not about being right or wrong; we're always looking for that "better way" in everything we do.

  4. 04

    Integrity

    Striving for personal excellence.

    Giving everything we do our very best, being true to ourselves and everyone with whom we come into contact. It's about being whole, honest, and principled — even when no one is watching.

  5. 05

    Simplicity

    Simplify to amplify.

    We remove friction, streamline processes, and communicate clearly to show what really matters — for ourselves, our team, and our customers. We simplify to amplify.
